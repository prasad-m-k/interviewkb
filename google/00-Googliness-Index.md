# Google Googliness & Leadership — Interview Prep Index

> STAR = Situation, Task, Action, Result
> **The Bottom Line:** Your decision. Your reasoning. Your outcome.

Google does not score against a fixed list of "principles" the way Amazon does. Interviewers rate every behavioral answer against four evaluation areas, and probe for a consistent set of traits regardless of which area the question targets. This folder converts the existing [[amazon/00-LP-Index|Amazon Leadership Principles]] story bank into that framework — same real career stories, reframed for what a Google interviewer is actually listening for.

## Level Calibration: Staff/Principal (L6/L7)

Every story in G01–G07 now carries two additions, calibrated to L6/L7 scope:

- **Quantified impact** — a concrete metric (latency, cost, hours saved, adoption count) attached to the outcome. Where the real number wasn't tracked at the time, it's marked **illustrative estimate** rather than stated as fact. Replace these with real telemetry, survey data, or manager-confirmed figures before using any of them in a live loop — an interviewer who probes "how do you know" deserves a real answer, and Google's own Collaboration/Earn-Trust bar penalizes a number you can't defend.
- **Why this reads L6/L7** — a one-line note naming the scope signal an interviewer is listening for: work that produced a reusable system/framework (not a one-off fix), a multiplier effect on other people's output, cross-team adoption without formal authority, or a judgment call made with no one available to check it.
