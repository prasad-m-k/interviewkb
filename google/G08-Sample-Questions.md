## Coverage gap

**"Give an example of when you delegated effectively"** has no strong story in the current bank — every story here is framed as *you* doing the work, not distributing it. Formal delegation (assigning ownership to someone else, defining the check-in cadence, and describing what you did with what came back) is worth building out as a dedicated story before a real loop, since Google will ask a version of this under Leadership.

## Notes for the loop

- Google interviewers use STAR, not SOAR — drop the SOAR variant when actually answering; it's kept in the source [[amazon/00-LP-Index|Amazon files]] for reference only.
- Always close with the "Decision · Reasoning · Outcome" framing explicitly if the interviewer doesn't draw it out themselves — it directly answers Google's stated bottom line: *your decision, your reasoning, your outcome.*
- For "hard" ambiguity-flagged questions (influence without authority, incomplete information), lead with the reasoning trail before the outcome — Google's General Cognitive Ability scoring cares more about visible reasoning than a clean resolution.
- At L6/L7, close every Result with a number, not just a description — "prevented a multi-region outage" is a mid-level answer; "prevented an outage that would have hit 3 regions and cost an estimated 10–15 engineer-hours per incident in blind triage" is a Staff-level answer. Every story in G01–G07 now has a **Quantified impact** line for this — verify each one against real data before the loop, since estimates currently marked illustrative are placeholders, not verified figures.
