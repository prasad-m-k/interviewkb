## STAR Format

**Situation:** During my research on the Escalation Threshold Theory (SET model), I developed a framework for how escalation dynamics work in sociotechnical systems. The framework borrowed heavily from Professor Robert A. Pape's work on escalation in international conflict. Using academic frameworks outside their original domain is common, but attribution matters — both ethically and for credibility.

**Task:** I needed to publish the paper and a related Medium series in a way that was intellectually honest about where the framework originated, while also making clear what my original contribution was. The temptation to soften the attribution and let readers assume the model was purely original was real.

**Action:** I included an explicit closing attribution paragraph in the Medium series crediting Pape by name for the nomenclature and framing I adapted, while clearly articulating where my contribution diverged: the application of the model to technical and organizational escalation contexts, the addition of the AI agent cascade indicator (TPI-AI), and the operationalization of the SET model for engineering environments. I did not hide the borrowed foundation. I built on it transparently and explained the extension.

**Result:** The attribution strengthened the paper's credibility rather than weakening it. Readers who know Pape's work recognized the foundation immediately and could assess my extension on its merits. The intellectual honesty established the kind of research character that matters for the EB1A peer review and judging criteria I am building toward.

## SOAR Format

**Situation:** The SET escalation framework drew on well-established academic work by a named scholar. Publishing without clear attribution would have been a credibility risk — detectable by anyone familiar with the source material.

**Obstacle:** Explicit attribution could make the work look derivative rather than original. The commercial pressure in academic writing often pushes authors toward burying sources and overclaiming novelty.

**Action:** I included a named attribution paragraph in both the research paper and the Medium series. I framed the relationship accurately: borrowed nomenclature, original application, and a clearly stated extension (the TPI-AI indicator) that Pape's original framework did not address.

**Result:** The transparency strengthened the work. The original contribution was easier to evaluate because the foundation was clearly stated. The approach reflects the standard I hold myself to as an independent researcher, and it aligns with the peer review credibility I need for the EB1A petition.
