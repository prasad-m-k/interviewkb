# LP12 - Deliver Results

> Leaders focus on the key inputs for their business and deliver them with the right quality and in a timely manner. Despite setbacks, they rise to the occasion and never settle.

---

## STAR Format

**Situation:** Building an EB1A extraordinary ability petition as an independent applicant — without immigration counsel, without a university affiliation, and while working full-time as a Solution Architect at Wipro/PNC — required sustained execution across multiple workstreams simultaneously over roughly twelve months.

**Task:** The petition required evidence across multiple USCIS criteria. The judging and peer review criterion was the hardest to build from scratch with no established academic profile. I had to create the publication record, the peer review history, the civic contributions, and the conference speaking opportunities concurrently, not sequentially.

**Action:** I executed across every front in parallel. I submitted four SSRN working papers under my independent researcher identity, each targeting a different criterion angle. I pursued conference speaking opportunities through DevOpsDays events and Sessionize. I identified and engaged with hackathon judging platforms (UC Davis Big Bang!, lablab.ai, Major League Hacking). I maintained my civic involvement with 100 Black Men and Suvidha as documented contribution. I managed IP separation carefully to preserve personal ownership of research outputs under California Labor Code Section 2870. I also documented each activity with EB1A evidentiary standards in mind from the start.

**Result:** Within twelve months: four SSRN-indexed papers, one SSIR-targeted article, an agentic system at patent-disclosure stage, a working voice interview agent, a published book, an active Medium publication, conference speaking submissions in flight, and a congressional support letter template drafted. The petition portfolio is substantive, documented, and defensible across multiple USCIS criteria.

## SOAR Format

**Situation:** An EB1A petition requires demonstrated extraordinary ability across multiple independent evidence categories. Building that record from a standing start, without institutional backing and while working full-time, required treating the petition as a multi-year product delivery program.

**Obstacle:** Every workstream competed for the same finite time. Research, civic work, conference engagement, IP management, and technical delivery at PNC all ran in parallel. There was no slack in the schedule and no one else to delegate to.

**Action:** I treated the EB1A petition the way I would treat a complex delivery program: prioritized by impact, executed in parallel where possible, and documented with the end reviewer in mind at every step. I maintained separation between Wipro work and independent research to protect IP ownership. I built the evidence base incrementally, with each output designed to serve multiple criteria simultaneously where feasible.

**Result:** The petition portfolio now spans published research, civic contributions, conference speaking, patent-stage architecture, a published book, and a public writing record. Each element is documented to evidentiary standard. The work was done without institutional support, on personal time, and without sacrificing the day-job delivery at PNC.
